# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, publishing private information, and conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `jiangjingzhe2004@gmail.com`. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
